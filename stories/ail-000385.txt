As someone who is visually impaired, the journey to writing my first book and managing a Blind Awareness Facebook page and website seemed daunting. Traditional tools often lack the adaptability needed for someone with severe vision loss, but ChatGPT changed everything for me. It became an indispensable writing partner. When fatigue or visual strain made it impossible to stare at a screen to edit grammar and pacing, I used voice dictation with ChatGPT to review chapters, restructure dialogue, and maintain thematic consistency. It didn't write the story for me—the ideas, characters, and lived disability experiences were mine—but it removed the physical barrier between my mind and the page. Because of it, my book is finished and published.
