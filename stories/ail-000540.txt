ChatGPT helped me find a song I hadn't heard in 20 years with almost zero concrete information

For two decades, I had this fragment of a melancholic indie song stuck in my head from the early 2000s. I didn't know the artist, the title, or any exact lyrics—just the general instrumentation (acoustic guitar with sparse synthesizer), that the singer had a frail high-pitched male voice, and a rough memory of the album art having muted pastel colors.

I spent years typing vague queries into Google, SoundHound, forums, and Reddit to no avail. 

I gave ChatGPT a brain dump of everything I remembered: the vague melody progression, the era (2002-2006), the emotional vibe, and the fact that it sounded like a mix of Bright Eyes and The Postal Service. After 3 back-and-forth guesses, it asked: "Could it be the artist Okay (Marty Anderson) and the song 'Give Up' from the album High Road?"

I pulled it up on YouTube and instantly broke down crying. That was it. Twenty years of searching solved in under 5 minutes.
