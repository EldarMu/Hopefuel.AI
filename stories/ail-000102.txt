@Midwest_Caregiver_55
My 82-year-old mother with early-stage vascular dementia has had an in-home companion for the past eighteen months. Mom kept complaining that her checking balance seemed low, but whenever I glanced at her monthly bank PDF, it just looked like normal grocery runs, pharmacy trips, and gas station charges.

Nobody has time to manually cross-reference 400 pages of bank transactions and utility bills spanning three years across two different banks.

I exported all 36 monthly CSVs and PDF statements and dumped them into ChatGPT's Advanced Data Analysis tool. I prompted: "Analyze these transactions for recurring anomalies, micro-charges just under $100, off-hour ATM withdrawals, and duplicate grocery runs within a 48-hour window."

In under two minutes, it found the pattern. The caregiver had been making duplicate $75-$95 cashback withdrawals at a local grocery store three times a week, always thirty minutes before her shift ended, totaling over $14,000 across sixteen months. I took the generated summary spreadsheet straight to our elder law attorney and the local adult protective services unit.
