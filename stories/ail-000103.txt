When Child Protective Services opened an investigation and placed my six-year-old in temporary kinship care with my aunt, they handed me a twelve-page "Family Reunification Safety and Case Plan."

I have severe dyslexia and only finished the 9th grade. The document was filled with legal mandates, court deadlines, acronyms like FSP, UA screenings, psych evaluations, and parenting compliance milestones. I couldn't read half the words, and every time I asked the caseworker, she spoke in rapid jargon and told me it was all in the packet. When you are poor and terrified of losing your child forever, asking them to read the paper to you makes you look incompetent.

I had my cousin photograph every single page and feed it into ChatGPT. I told it: "Turn this into a simple weekly checklist. Tell me where I have to be, what time, what paperwork I must bring, and what words to never say in these sessions."

It organized all twelve pages into a clean, day-by-day binder plan. I attended every single required appointment, passed every screening, and submitted the exact compliance logs required. Case closed last Friday. I got them back.
