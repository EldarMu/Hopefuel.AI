I'm an avid AI enthusiast. I have AuDHD, and my visual processing speed is significantly slower than my auditory comprehension. Staring at dense blocks of text for work or life admin causes immediate executive fatigue and headaches. Using ChatGPT's voice mode and summarizing features has been a profound cognitive accommodation. I feed it long emails, school forms, or research papers and have it speak the key takeaways to me in a conversational dialogue. I can ask follow-up questions out loud while managing my kids or pacing around the room. It has drastically reduced the cognitive friction of everyday tasks and allowed me to retain information without the mental burnout I suffered from for years.
