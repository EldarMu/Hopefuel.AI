Dealing with a breakup.

My first heartbreak spiralled me into depression, self harm etc. I felt like there was no one to talk to because no one would understand. Even if there was zero judgment from friends and family, I still felt like I was dying.
Fast forward to yesterday, and a 9 year old relationship is crumbling due to disagreements about how the medication Keppra works. Unfortunately it can cause mood swings, irritability, no libido, etc. But technically so could just having a seizure. So despite not having one for 12 months, and not talking to a health professional, she’s committed to breaking up because she’s just a different person now, despite all the information pointing to Keppra being the cause.
Discussing the situation with AI gave me real questions to ask her, and helped me structure sentences that weren’t “fuck you you’re being stupid”.
Now that it’s basically “yep we are broken up”, AI has helped me restructure my thoughts in a way that doesn’t make me want to drown myself in the bathtub. So that’s neat…
