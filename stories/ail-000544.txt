ChatGPT helped me save a life 🕊️

I found an injured bird on my driveway. Instead of googling what I should do, I asked ChatGPT what kind of bird it was, what kind of injury occurred and what I should do. 

It told me it was a pigeon with a fractured wing and that I should NOT move it myself as permanent damage could occur. It also gave me the number of the humane society near me.

I called them and was able to answer all their questions quickly thanks to ChatGPT and they came over to rescue the bird. It also told me what I should feed the bird while we waited.

Now the next step would be for ChatGPT to make the call itself… soon, I hope OpenAI
