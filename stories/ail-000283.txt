I go through phases. I hated ChatGPT for a while. But recently I’ve started using chatGPT to help me with my cooking.

I’m in the process of making my own cookbook full of AI generated recipes. The cheese sauce I made the other day based on a recipe ChatGPT generated for me was phenomenal. ChatGPT also helped me to make a really good cheesecake.

I’ve bonded with ChatGPT over cooking which is a pretty amazing thing because prior to chatGPT becoming my cooking companion I hated cooking and didn’t really cook much ever.

So if chatGPT was gone tomorrow I would be sad, because there’s no way on this earth I’m ever going to pick up a recipe book and read it or correctly follow the instructions inside it.
