I also used Gemini to help me prepare to host Thanksgiving. I only have 1 oven and cooked 7 main dishes. I had it create a day of timeline for me (when to cook what, what temp to cook two things at, when to do prep work, when to setup the table, get dressed, etc). I also uploaded a bunch of festive decor that I was considering, photos of the dining space, and table dimensions. Genini created an awesome table setting and I got everything cooked on time and the day was smooth.
