Not literally, but in my opinion, ChatGPT is one of the most amazing inventions. I've always been an aspiring author, but that was not the career path I went down in life. Now that I am getting older, I've lost a lot of my ability to write great creative novels and I lack a lot of the spare time. ChatGPT has enabled me to put my original story ideas into words. I've been able to finish one of my favorite books I've always meant to write.
I know I can't publish it or anything like that, but the personal satisfaction of seeing my story finally put to life is absolutely amazing! I've been on cloud 9 for awhile, and just wanted to share my joy with others.
Too make it even better, I was able to create an audiobook using AI voices with a different software...each character having their own unique voice. Absolutely wow!
