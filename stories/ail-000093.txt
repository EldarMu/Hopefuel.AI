TIL: how to get ChatGPT to help my son with dyslexia and ADHD read

Tl/dr I tought chatGPT about Bionic Reading methods.  Then got it to write stories we made up using that method for my dyslexic son

So my son has learning difficulties when it comes to reading.  He finds it really difficult to read and uses a lot of energy to read simple text.

I’ve seen a few things recently on bionic reading and gave it a go on CHATGPT.  Didn’t work at first, it was bolding whole words.  So I tought it more about the method, copy pasta details from internet resources.

Then my son picked a few funny themes for the story to be about.  Then read me the story.  He loved it as it created his story, and wanted to read.  Out put was great for him.

Sharing idea for other parents in case it could be helpful
