I use ChatGPT whenever my spouse and I get into a heated argument and I feel myself getting overwhelmed or defensive. Instead of reacting impulsively in the moment, I take 10 minutes to step away, write out everything I'm feeling and what was said into ChatGPT, and ask it to help me process the emotion objectively and draft a calm, constructive response focused on de-escalation and understanding.

It acts like a non-judgmental communication coach that helps me separate my emotional triggers from the actual issue. When I come back to the conversation, we're able to talk through the problem rationally without spiraling into a fight. It's genuinely transformed how we handle conflict in our marriage.
