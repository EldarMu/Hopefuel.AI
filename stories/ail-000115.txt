My husband has been ill this year. It's helped me know when was appropriate to take him to hospital (you know what it's like, you can start underplaying symptoms in your head, convince yourself they're not as serious as they are etc).

When hospitalised for over 2 and a half weeks it diagnosed him with a very rare autoimmune condition a week before the doctors did due to the sheer amount of information I put into it. It has continuously helped me when I've asked questions about the condition and symptoms etc since then, including when I should be contacting the consultant.

It's helped me order my thoughts and put them in the best way possible when completing the PIP form for my husband, and also the ESA form. I did a blue badge application for him which was unsuccessful because I didn't put enough detail in, I then used AI to help me with the appeal and was contacted in under 48 hours to say it had been successful.

I don't take everything it says as gospel, and doing the PIP form certainly exposed some of it's shortcomings, which I had to point out to it more than once. But as long as you accept all of that and check what it tells you, it can be an extremely useful tool. I often know what I want to say when it comes to forms, applications etc, but have trouble expressing it. Chatgpt has helped me massively with that.
