ChatGPT helped me get married to my crush

I was hopelessly in love with someone for over a year but had terrible social anxiety and constantly overthought every interaction. Whenever we texted, I would get completely paralyzed trying to figure out how to respond without seeming desperate or distant.

I started pasting our text threads into ChatGPT and asking it: "Help me reply warmly, authentically, and show interest while maintaining healthy emotional boundaries." It gave me perspective, helped me understand communication subtext, and prevented me from self-sabotaging when misunderstandings arose. 

Things progressed naturally, he proposed two months later, and our families celebrated our wedding last month. AI basically served as the wisest, most level-headed big sister I never had.
