I have depression and spent a decade failing to build an idea. AI helped me finally ship it.

I've been wanting to build a social site for TV shows and movies since the early '10s. For ten years, every time I tried to learn full-stack development, depressive episodes and chronic fatigue would derail my progress, leaving dozens of abandoned Git repositories and compounding my feelings of worthlessness.

Using Claude and Gemini together changed everything. I maintained the product vision, database schema design, and feature roadmaps, while AI acted as the tireless senior engineer handling the boilerplate, API routes, and React state management.

Last night, after ten years of believing I would never amount to anything, I deployed the live web application to production and welcomed our first 100 users. It completely restored my belief in myself.
