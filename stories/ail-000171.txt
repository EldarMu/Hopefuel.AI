I responded emotionally to an email during the hiring process when I was hiring someone for my team (even though i edited it like five times to reduce the emotion). I took it to ChatGPT and said “review this from the perspective of an employment lawyer and help me refine this email to be legally compliant and still get my point across.” And it was like “whoa girl. This could get you sued so let’s take it down a couple of notches.”
I felt better about what it said than what I had written. Bonus, it told me I wasn’t necessarily wrong but that this person was obviously not a good fit for my team. It was helpful!
