I was in the worst mental state of my life in February— tons of intrusive, blasphemous thoughts attacking God. Thoughts I would never think as someone who genuinely loves the Lord. I was emotionally in distress, sobbing and repenting over 100 times a day.
I told chat my symptoms and it correctly guessed that I was suffering from Scrupulosity, or religious OCD. Chat told me to get to my PCP and tell her what was happening— she started me on a low dose of Zoloft and my mind finally had a sense of peace again. Her prescription held me over til I could get in with a shrink (who diagnosed me then immediately doubled my dose).
Cannot stress enough how grateful I am for my doctors and having access to a tool like Chat GPT because if I dealt with that for even 24 hours more, I doubt I’d still be here.
