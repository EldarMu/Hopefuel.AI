After four years of being told "periods are just uncomfortable" and "you have IBS," I finally got referred for an exploratory laparoscopy and ultrasound mapping today.

Nothing about my body changed, only the grammar.

I fed all my medical journal entries, pain logs, and past dismissal notes into ChatGPT and told it: "Doctors keep writing down that I am an anxious female with fatigue. Translate my real physical experience into the clinical vocabulary of surgical gynecologists."

It told me not to say 'tired'—it told me to describe functional impairment. It told me not to say 'bad cramps'—it taught me to specify cyclical dyschezia, localized non-radiating pelvic pain at the left sacroiliac joint, and pain refractory to 800mg Ibuprofen. I wrote it down the way it suggested on a 3x5 index card.

I handed the card to the specialist at the beginning of the appointment. She read it, looked up, did a targeted physical exam, and immediately ordered a pelvic MRI with endometriosis protocol. Same symptoms, different words, and suddenly she listened.
