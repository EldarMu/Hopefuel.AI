ChatGPT is a Lifesaver for Me as a Disabled User, but Sometimes It Feels Like a Double-Edged Sword

I wanted to share my experience with ChatGPT and how it has been a tremendous help and time saver for me. As someone with a disability that makes manually typing/writing and wording posts challenging, ChatGPT has been invaluable. It assists me in articulating my thoughts clearly and efficiently, allowing me to participate more actively in various online communities.

However, I've encountered some frustrating issues. People often call me out for "sounding like an AI," and I've even gotten into trouble on subreddits that forbid the use of AI-generated content. This is incredibly disheartening because I feel like I'm a prime example of someone who benefits greatly from this technology. Instead of feeling supported, I'm often made to feel like I'm cheating or being dishonest.

Does anyone else face similar challenges? How do you handle these situations? I'd love to hear your thoughts and any advice you might have.

Thanks for listening!
